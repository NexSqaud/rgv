## Executive Summary

An audit of the Recipe Graph Viewer updates was conducted against the `antislop` design, craftsmanship, and code hygiene standards.

The updates address all requested fixes:
1. Fixed recipe selection on graph ingredient click without abruptly closing the menu.
2. Relocated NEI's graph button to sit inside the NEI recipe window next to the transfer button.
3. Implemented full JEI support in 1.12.2 for adding recipes to the craft graph directly from `RecipesGui`.
4. Implemented cross-session persistence for craft graph tabs and recipe assignments in `config/rgv_tabs.cfg`.
5. Upgraded button visuals to vanilla sliced `widgets.png` textures with a custom graph icon replacing text.
